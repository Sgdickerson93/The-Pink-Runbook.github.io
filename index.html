03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROMPT

Technical curiosity.
People-first thinking.

I’m Shelby Dickerson, a mid-level Support Engineer on Deloitte Consulting’s Customer Success team and a Cloud and Network Engineering student at WGU, focusing on AWS. I bring nearly 10 years of experience in technology, beginning with a part-time Application Development internship at IBM during my final semester of college. My background centers on technical support and client services: understanding problems, troubleshooting thoughtfully, and helping people move forward with confidence. Today, I’m building on that foundation through cloud engineering and DevOps. I’m curious about how systems work, what keeps them reliable, and how clear communication supports both the technology and the people using it.

The Pink Runbook is where I document that journey: hands-on labs, troubleshooting notes, and lessons from each iteration. My goal is to pair technical care with a client experience that feels calm and clear.
